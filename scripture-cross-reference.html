How to use cross-references well

  1. Read the full paragraph or chapter around your starting verse.
  2. Open each related passage and read it in its own setting.
  3. Ask whether the connection is a quotation, shared theme, parallel account, or similar wording.
  4. Notice differences as well as similarities; a shared word does not automatically mean two passages teach the same thing.
