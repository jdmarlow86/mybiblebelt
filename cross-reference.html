A simple way to study cross-references

  1. Read the main passage and note its audience, setting, and surrounding argument.
  2. Open related passages and check whether they share a subject, quote, image, or wording.
  3. Notice both the connections and the differences; don't assume similar words always mean the same thing.
  4. Compare translations if useful, and let the context of each passage guide your conclusion.
