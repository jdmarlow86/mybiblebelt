Prophecy & End Times

"Blessed is he that readeth, and they that hear the words of this prophecy" - Revelation 1:3

Why Study Prophecy?

Prophecy is not given to satisfy curiosity about the future, but to prepare us for what is coming. Daniel and Revelation contain important prophetic visions that Christians have studied for centuries. Studying prophecy can help us:

  • Recognize the times we live in
  • Prepare for Christ's return
  • Understand the Great Controversy between Christ and Satan
  • Find hope in God's ultimate victory

Daniel 2 - The Great Image

King Nebuchadnezzar's dream of a great image reveals the succession of world empires:

  1. Head of Gold: Babylon (605-539 BC)
  2. Chest of Silver: Medo-Persia (539-331 BC)
  3. Belly of Brass: Greece (331-168 BC)
  4. Legs of Iron: Rome (168 BC - 476 AD)
  5. Feet of Iron and Clay: Divided Europe (476 AD - Present)
  6. The Stone: God's everlasting kingdom (Future)

"And in the days of these kings shall the God of heaven set up a kingdom, which shall never be destroyed... it shall break in pieces and consume all these kingdoms, and it shall stand for ever." - Daniel 2:44

Daniel 7 - The Four Beasts

Daniel's vision of four beasts corresponds to the metals in Daniel 2:

  • Lion with eagle's wings: Babylon
  • Bear with three ribs: Medo-Persia
  • Leopard with four heads: Greece (Alexander's divided kingdom)
  • Dreadful beast with iron teeth: Rome
  • Little Horn: A power that speaks against God and persecutes the saints; interpretations of its historical identity vary.

The judgment scene in Daniel 7:9-14 shows the heavenly court where "the Ancient of days" sits in judgment, and "one like the Son of man" receives dominion.

Revelation - The Unveiling of Jesus Christ

Key themes in Revelation:

  • Chapters 1-3: Messages to the seven churches (church history)
  • Chapters 4-5: The throne room of God
  • Chapters 6-18: The seven seals, trumpets, and plagues
  • Chapters 12-14: The Great Controversy - Satan's war against God's people
  • Chapters 19-22: Christ's return, millennium, and new earth

"And I saw a new heaven and a new earth: for the first heaven and the first earth were passed away; and there was no more sea." - Revelation 21:1

The Three Angels' Messages

Revelation 14:6-12 contains God's final message to the world before Christ's return:

First Angel: "Fear God, and give glory to him; for the hour of his judgment is come: and worship him that made heaven, and earth, and the sea, and the fountains of waters." (Revelation 14:7) - Call to worship the Creator

Second Angel: "Babylon is fallen, is fallen, that great city, because she made all nations drink of the wine of the wrath of her fornication." (Revelation 14:8) - Warning about fallen religious systems

Third Angel: Warning against the mark of the beast and calling for "patience of the saints" who "keep the commandments of God, and the faith of Jesus." (Revelation 14:12)

Signs of Christ's Return

Jesus gave clear signs of His return in Matthew 24:

  • False christs and false prophets (v. 5, 24)
  • Wars and rumors of wars (v. 6)
  • Famines, pestilences, earthquakes (v. 7)
  • Persecution of believers (v. 9)
  • Lawlessness abounding (v. 12)
  • Gospel preached to all nations (v. 14)

"Watch therefore: for ye know not what hour your Lord doth come." - Matthew 24:42

The Sabbath in Prophecy

These passages invite readers to study the relationship between God's commandments, worship, and faithfulness. Christians differ on how these prophecies relate to the Sabbath and end-time events:

"And he shall speak great words against the most High, and shall wear out the saints of the most High, and think to change times and laws: and they shall be given into his hand until a time and times and the dividing of time." - Daniel 7:25

Revelation describes God's people as keeping His commandments and remaining faithful to Jesus (Revelation 12:17; 14:12). Compare these passages with Exodus 20:8-11 and Isaiah 56:2, and consider how different Christian traditions interpret their connection.

"Blessed is the man that doeth this, that keepeth the sabbath from polluting it, and keepeth his hand from doing any evil." - Isaiah 56:2
