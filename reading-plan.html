A simple way to read each passage

  1. Observe: What does the passage actually say? Notice repeated words, people, and actions.
  2. Understand: Read the surrounding verses. Ask who is speaking, to whom, and why.
  3. Reflect: What does this passage show about God, people, or faithful living?
  4. Respond: Write one question, prayer, or practical step. It is fine to leave questions unanswered for now.

Reading plans are guides, not a test. Take more than one day when a passage needs careful attention.
